## The Real Difference Nobody Talks About

Look, I could give you a feature comparison table. Mare has this, Savee has that. But you've seen those before, and they don't help you decide.

Here's what actually matters: how you work.

### When Mare Makes Sense

If you're the kind of person who likes meticulous organization, Mare might work for you.

I worked with a designer last year who swore by Mare. Her process was thorough. Every reference tagged, every project documented. She spent maybe 2 hours a week just organizing. And you know what? It worked for her. She could find anything in 30 seconds.

But that's not how most of us work.

### When Savee Actually Wins

Most designers I know? They're visual thinkers. They remember "that project with the blue gradient" or "the typography from that restaurant branding." They don't think in tags and folders. They think in vibes.

That's where Savee clicks.

### What About Mare?

Full disclosure: I helped build Mare. But here's why I'm mentioning it - we made it for the messy middle.

You don't want to be a librarian. But you also don't want to scroll endlessly through an algorithm feed that shows you what it thinks you want.

Mare gives you:
- Visual organization that matches how your brain works
- Fast retrieval without the tagging overhead

### The Honest Verdict

If you're all-in on Mare's approach, stick with it. If Savee vibes with your workflow, that's cool too.

But if you're tired of choosing between obsessive organization and chaos? Maybe try something built for how designers actually think.
